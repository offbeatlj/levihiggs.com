ISSUE NO. 01 — THE ANATOMY OF PERSEVERANCE • DUAL-INBOX LONGITUDINAL ARCHIVE

Two thousand three hundred and forty-two job emails, stripped to their statistical truth.

Every career trajectory is publicly summarized by its destinations, while the thousands of outbound applications, automated rejections, silent non-responses, and multi-round interview loops remain hidden inside private inboxes. This study compiles every job-related email across two personal Gmail accounts—scrubbed of all recruiter PII, personal names, and calendar years—to document the true ratio of human effort to outcome.

METHODOLOGY & SANITIZATION
All personal names, email addresses, phone numbers, and calendar years have been algorithmically redacted. Cyclical timestamps (Month-of-Year, Day-of-Week, Hour-of-Day) are preserved to study hiring seasonality.
